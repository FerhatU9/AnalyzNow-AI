02 / DATA TRANSPARENCY

Know what you're looking at.

A good analytical system makes its assumptions and limitations visible.

01 / PROVENANCE

Fictional by design.

Six Demo clubs and artificial match results are included in the public repository. No restricted real-match data or bookmaker feeds are used.

02 / TIME AWARENESS

No future leakage.

The historical cutoff is applied before fitting the model. Only matches strictly before the selected date contribute to the calculation.

03 / MODEL HONESTY

Limits stay visible.

The independent-Poisson method does not incorporate injuries, expected goals, tactics, or correlations between scorelines. This website does not claim verified accuracy on current fixtures.
